About Me

Dr. Yushuai Li is an assistiant professor in Department of Computer Science at Aalborg University. His main research interests include distributed optimization and control, machine learning, digital twin, and their ap plications in integrated energy and transportation systems. He has published more than 100 papers in prestigious journals and conferences. Two of them are “Hot Papers and seven of them are "Highly Cited Papers". He has an h-index of 28 and 3800+ citations according to Google scholar. He received the Best Paper Awards from Journal of Modern Power Systems and Clean Energy (MPCE) in 2021 and 2024, and ICCSIE in 2023, IEEE EI2 in 2024. He received the Excellent Young Expert Award from MPCE (2023), the CAA Second Prize of National Natural Science (2022), and the CAA First Prize of National Natural Science (2023). He serves as Associate Editors for IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering, MPCE, IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Magazine, IET Cyber-Physical Systems: Theory & Applications, and cyber-physical energy systems. He served as the PHD Thesis Competition Chair for IEEE SmartGridComm 2024 and 2025.

Research Interests

My research focuses on smart, safe, and sustainable machine learning for decision-making in cyber-physical systems (CPSs). Specifically, my current research interests are as follows:

  • Reinforcement learning (e.g., single-agent, multi-agent, safety-aware)
  • Deep learning (e.g., contrastive learning and graph neural networks)
  • Control theory (e.g., Lyapunov stability theory and consensus theory)
  • Digital energy and digital transportation
